Japan's Anime Culture - Akhibara

Akhibara is the number one district in Tokyo for all anime things. This area has lot of cafes that are inspired by anime culture. Akhibara has Pokemon Centers, where fans take part in card trading, card batteles, and explore the array of Pokemon gifts. They have the Asagaya anime street, where the street is dedicated to anime-inspired memorabilia, clothing, comics, cafes, and souvenirs.

Mount Fuji

Mount Fuji is the highest mountain in Japan, standing at 3776 meters tall. It is an
active stratovolcano with a symmetrical conical shape. It sits on the border of
the Yamanashi and Shizuka on Honhu Island.
